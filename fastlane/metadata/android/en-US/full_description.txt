Follow stocks and understand your portfolio at a glance.

MarketMonk is a stock tracker and portfolio monitor for investors who want a clear view of market prices, holdings, and performance. Add the shares you follow, build your portfolio, and explore price charts from one straightforward dashboard.

FEATURES

• Track stock prices and market performance
• Build and monitor a personal investment portfolio
• View line charts to explore price movements and gains
• Organise the information that matters to you
• Adjust settings to suit your preferred investing view

MarketMonk is designed to make portfolio tracking easier to read, whether you are checking a watchlist or reviewing your investments.

Market data is provided for information only and is not financial advice. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.
